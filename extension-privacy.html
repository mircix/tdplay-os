TDPlay OS — Instagram side panel

Privacy policy · last updated 6 October 2026
The extension collects nothing. It stores no personal data, sends nothing to any server of ours, and has no analytics. There is no account and nothing to opt out of.

What it does

The extension shows instagram.com — and, on request, TDPlay OS — inside Chrome's side panel. Instagram sends headers that stop other pages from displaying it; inside its own panel the extension removes those headers so the page renders. That rule is limited to frames that belong to no browser tab, which is what a side panel is, so Instagram keeps its protection on every website you visit.

What it stores

  • Chrome session storage, cleared when Chrome closes: which Instagram page the panel should show, when you click Instagram in TDPlay OS. Nothing else, and it never leaves your browser.
  • You are signed in to Instagram as yourself, through Chrome's own cookies for instagram.com. The extension does not read, copy or transmit them.

Who it talks to

Only instagram.com (the page in the panel) and tdplay.site / mircix.github.io (TDPlay OS, when you put it in the panel). TDPlay OS may ask the extension two things: whether it is installed, and to open the panel on an Instagram page. No other site can talk to it.

Permissions, and why

  • Side panel — the panel is the whole point.
  • Access to instagram.com — to show it in the panel and drop the framing headers there.
  • Access to mircix.github.io — so TDPlay OS can ask the panel to open.
  • Storage — the one session value above.
